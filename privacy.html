Privacy at a glance

Animal Puzzle Fun World

This page summarizes how privacy works for the Animal Puzzle Fun World app on Apple devices. It is written for families and casual players and avoids unnecessary technical jargon.

What this notice covers

This notice applies to the Animal Puzzle Fun World mobile application obtained through the Apple App Store. It describes our approach to personal information in plain language and complements Apple’s own privacy labels shown on the store listing for your installed version.

Collection of personal data

Animal Puzzle Fun World is built so that we do not collect personal data from you through the app for our own use. We do not ask you to create an in-app account for basic puzzle play, and we do not use this app to gather names, email addresses, or similar identifiers from players.

Summary: the experience is designed around offline-friendly puzzle fun without pulling personal information into our systems through the app.

Information on your device

Like many puzzle games, some preferences or progress may be stored only on your device so the app can remember your session. That local information stays under your device controls unless you remove the app or clear app data using system settings.

Apple & the App Store

Downloading, updating, and billing are handled by Apple. Apple may process certain technical and account-related information according to its own policies and your device settings. You can review privacy controls in iOS or iPadOS Settings and on your Apple ID account page.

Younger players

The app is rated for broad audiences. Parents and guardians should use Apple’s parental controls and Screen Time features if they want to manage installs, purchases, or play time for children using a shared device.

Updates to this summary

If features or platform rules change in a way that affects privacy, we may revise this page. The version shown here reflects our current intended practices for Animal Puzzle Fun World; always compare with the App Store privacy details for the exact build on your device.

For the official App Store summary of data practices and the latest app information, open Animal Puzzle Fun World on the App Store on your device or visit the public listing through your browser.
